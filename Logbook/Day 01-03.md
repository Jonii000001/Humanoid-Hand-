Day 1-3:

In the first days, I just made a plan of what I want to build and how I'm going to start it all. I watched a bunch of videos, documentaries, looked at images, and read through sheets as inspiration.

Then I made my own creation and tried to make a raw sketch of what I want to build. I looked at several different mechanisms, and I think the tendon-driven mechanism would be perfect, because I can place the Servomotors in the arm and wrist, so there won't be too much mass in the palm. The only disadvantage is that a tendon system can be inaccurate in the movements of the fingers.

To explain the mechanism simply, you have to think about a servomotor, do you know? The Servomotor has a spool on it with two spaces for the tendon. The one tendon side will be connected to one space of the spool in the clockwise direction, going up in front of the finger to pull the finger down to hold something like an apple. Then at the front, I will make a deflection up to the upper side of the finger, where the fingernail is. From there I'll go backwards to the Servomotor also trough the palm and back on the spool, but this time I will place the tendon into the other space against the clockwise direction

The future scope is to identify a mechanism for accurate angel reading and correcting, before the fingers move wrong.

The most difficult part is to make your own creation of a humanoid hand and find the perfect mechanism for accurate finger movement.
