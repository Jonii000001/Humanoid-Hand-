Day 4: Today I started sketching and constructing the joint and the spool for the Servomotor.

I planned today that I'll probably take the MG994/995, because it's budget-friendly for me and has the right torque, I think. However, if I can secure funding at the Macondo hack club, I don't know if I can, because it's the first time I've heard of that method.

My strategy for producing the parts is to buy a 3D printer with my own cash. Maybe I will buy me the Bambulab p1s or the Bambulab A1. I will need one to make the project in real life, because prototyping in a factory will be expensive. I also decided to take a PETG filament, because I think it's more resistant than Basic PLA, and it's more flexible, which is better for the joint.

In CAD, I constructed the joint with a long set screw and a screw nut to fix the screw. In the middle are two MR84ZZ ball bearings (they are very small and don't take too much space in the joints, so I took them). At the middle of the set screw is a V-Nut ball bearing where I can thread a tendon like the mechanism, which I described yesterday. You can see the V-Nut ball bearing and the joint in the pictures.
