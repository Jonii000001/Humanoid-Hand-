## Iteration 1
Today was my first attempt at a more advanced CAD design. I tried to design just the mechanism for the finger for the first time. The appearance was just my secondary importance. It took me a really long time, because I had to learn all the CAD tools first, so I watched some YouTube videos on how Autodesk Inventor works and which button does what.

I designed one big part, and in the end I combined everything in a Autodesk inventor assembly. I used a lot the mirror tool, because this was really useful, I didn't have to design it on the other side. I also didn't use the bearings from yesterday, but if I use this part, the humanoid hand won't look professional — it'll just look simple. I only made a big, hollow part so I can put the tendon through it, and at the joint there's a V-groove ball bearing on the set screw.

It wasn't an easy day for me, because at the beginning I wasn't even able to make a hole inside a part, so it was a real challenge.

Future: In the next few days I want to make the part cleaner and change some sizes, so I can use a screw nut at the end of the M2 set screw. I also have to refine the structure.
