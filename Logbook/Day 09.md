## Dimension correction

• Today I've redesigned the part from yesterday and changed the measurements to even dimensions. I also used for the first time the fixed tolerances tool in Autodesk Inventor, where you can set a dimension for a text and, when you want to use a tolerance, you can just write the word. I used a 0.15mm tolerance for the bearing housing and holes like the magnet hole. In addition, I only used a tolerance because of the shrinking during the 3D printing process, as the filament cools down and shrinks a bit.

• This time it was also easier to create the finger part, because I had an example of it from yesterday. I only had to change the dimensions, and of course, I used the mirror tool, so I had to design only one side of the part. I used the whole ball bearing housing (8mm OD) from the old one.

• Pending: I've to extrude (Sweeping tool) a long hole through the part for the routing of the tendon for the later pulley-system.
