## Custom PCB planning

Today, I took the basic AS5600 design from the internet and downloaded a free template for CAD. After that, I added it to an Autodesk Inventor Assembly file and saw that the basic design was much too big for the joint. That was the reason why I had to create my own PCB.

My plan is now to design a custom, minimized PCB that fits perfectly on my joint. I want to take the same schematic as the AS5600, but just place it near together, so I can reduce the board. I have to place the chip perfectly into the centre and I want them to integrate it into the part, because I'll create a housing, which I can open to install the chip and close to keep the PCB from moving around and misreading the axis. In my thoughts, I want to make a round PCB that is almost the same size as the joint, just a bit tinier, and then maybe screw it onto the part.
