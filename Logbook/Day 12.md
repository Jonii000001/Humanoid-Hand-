Day 12:

Today was the first time I designed a custom PCB and used KICAD. At the beginning of the day, I didn't have any clue which software I should use or how I would design a PCB. I had never done it before. At first, I had to watch some YouTube tutorials on how it works, but I didn't do that for long, because I just wanted to start and learn by doing.

In the next steps, I had to find the schematic symbols for the AS5600, because the basic AS5600 schematic was everywhere on the internet, so it wasn't as difficult to find a schematic for the board.

After a bunch of hours of problem-solving and struggling to get a functional PCB schematic (the schematic wasn't the most difficult part, the real small construction, where I had to put them all in a small case, was the difficult part), I completed it. I designed the PCB very well, because I wanted to have at the end a functional PCB long.

Even my dad was impressed that I had designed my own PCB, because it was my first time.

At the end, I measured the size of the CAD part, and I have perfectly tuned the PCB for the part. Of course, I put my name on the PCB. This was my first great success of the project.
