## Finishing PCB and fingertip (FSR)

Today I was really productive, I did a lot of things.

First, I checked the PCB design from yesterday, but I think everything is okay. After that I corrected the finger parts, so they don't collide each other and now everything works, expect the long first finger part collided with the other one.

Belong to that I redesigned the fingertip, for the FSR Sensor but I didn't change much, I only changed the size and the hole to connect the fingertip with a screw (M2 screw) together with the fingertip connection part. Now it fits perfectly.

After that I began to sketch another part for the finger, but I didn't get along, because it's very difficult, I didn't have an idea to move on. Maybe tomorrow I'll go on with that part.

I know it is a lot of work until I finish the humanoid hand, but I trust the progress. It's just a learning effect for me, because it's my first project I have done so far by myself.
