## Finger base part CAD

Today I finished the whole finger part, which I began yesterday (finger base part).

I used the same bearing-clip mechanism as before when designing the finger parts.

Furthermore, I copied just the ball bearing measurements and constructed the ball bearing clip housing.

I used the mirror tool very often, so it made the work easier.

For the people who were wondering why I designed the ball bearing holder, I said I would use diametral magnets at the joints. I know that, but I did it just for prototyping, because if I get a printer now, I could print it and assemble it; otherwise, I can't put a ball bearing on the joint.

Tomorrow I'll begin the last finger part to design, and then I'll integrate the PCB, and I'll look at how I'll connect the cables to the servomotor, so it doesn't have much resistance.

I'll also have to figure out how so many cables and tendons can go through the finger part.

I don't know yet how I should complete all the tasks, but everything is possible. I'll have to solve many problems until I'm finished. After that, I'll have to buy myself a 3D printer from my cash and try to print it, for prototyping, and try the mechanism if it works (I ordered MR84ZZ ball bearing, so I can try to assemble one finger).

Today I also came up with the thought that I will use PLA filament for fit dimensional verification and PETG for the part itself.
