## Ball bearing issues (ceramic???)

Today I was really disappointed, because I looked for almost 1 hour straight for a diametrically magnetized magnet (like yesterday), but I couldn't find any provider who sells the perfect fit for me. But suddenly I saw a shop that sells 4x4 magnets, which was a success for me......

Because of that, I don't have to use a bigger ball bearing, but I came to another problem. I couldn't use the normal MR84 ZZ, because it is made of metal and it would fool the signals from the 14-bit sensor (AS5048A), because of the ferromagnetic fields.

So, I had to look for another solution. I'm not quite sure, but I think I'll buy a ceramic ball bearing with the measurements of 4x9x4, because the magnet fits perfectly inside.

The main problem is that they are really expensive and I can't afford them on my own, so I have to think of a solution. I also changed the CAD part and deleted the ball bearing holder from the finger part; instead, I'll use the magnet because it has measurements for the hole perfectly. I also have to make the finger part a bit bigger, because I can't find an affordable ceramic bearing with 4x8x3 size, so I've to take the 4x9x4 bearing.
