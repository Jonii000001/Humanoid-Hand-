## PCB housing designing (new mechanism tried)

Today I made really big progress.

First of all, I designed the housing for the PCB with a canopy like that, with those little nubs, which I can then position perfectly in place. I don't know if I made the right decision for the housing, but I need also to the stick the PCB cover, or I'll use insulating tape for masking. I'll mind this up later.

But it's a really huge problem, the housing takes the joint's width up to 30cm and I think that's a bit too large for a human hand joint. Without the housing for the PCB, the joint would take a 21 cm width, and that would be perfect.

Later on, I connected all parts, which I had designed so far, and my first finger was ready (I need to design the housing for the base part of the finger, but I didn't want to do that, because the housing is a difficult problem for me now).

After that, I began with the palm, and it is very difficult, because I didn't know how I should design as realistic as a real human hand, so humanoid shapes are really difficult. By the way, I use Autodesk Inventor as a CAD program for my project, because I have a free school licence with my school email, luckily.

Tomorrow I'll go on with my project. I hope I will be able to finish the CAD thing so I can go on with much more exciting parts, because CAD is going to be boring, since I have done so much already. I also wait for the sales by Bambu Lab (Anniversary Sale ), and then I'll buy myself a 3d printer from my own cash. It hurts a bit that I have to spend so much money, but I want to do everything for my future goals and education (education is for me the most important thing in life for me)

Maybe later in the future, I'll make a version 2 of my humanoid hand, and then I want to correct every detail of the problems and optimize it to another level of humanoid hand, but now I want to go on and complete my first project.
