## Palm designing for torque reduction, and PCB cost looking

I redesigned the palm.

First, my idea was to make the hand palm a bit longer (realistic), but I ended up realizing that it would cost me more torque power for the motors, because of the lever law in physics. So, I kept it a bit shorter, but maybe later, with a few test tries, I'll change it to a more realistic palm shape. It should work first, before I do the previous improvement.

I also tried to make a better variation of the PCB housing, but I don't know yet if it works.

I'm still waiting for my 3d printer, the days when it arrives, I'll print everything and try prototyping. If something doesn't work, I'll have to change the CAD parts and also make all things at the end more precise.

This is only now a construction to simulate if everything works, and at the end, I'll improve it in more detail, if there is something better possible. I also looked today at how much the PCB would cost me to buy.
