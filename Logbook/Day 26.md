## Redesigning of the palm for the second time.

At first of this day, I completed all the finger connectors of the palm, except the thumb connector. It was very hard, because I tried nearly 50 different positions for the connectors.

At the end, I came to the point that the connectors are too close together, so I've to redesign it for the third time.

Today, I also made the example hand and assembled 4 fingers on it (I only used the middle part for the index finger part). I think it looks great for the first time assembled, but it's improvable.

Pending: I have to make a housing (extra parts to put it over the other ones) for everything so that it looks realistic.
