## Redesigning of the palm for the third time.

At first of this day, I made a redesign of the palm for the third time, but I didn't finish it yet, because I think it doesn't make any sense without all the fingers assembled.

Also, I have to make a small compatible housing for the PCB, which doesn't affect the other fingers. Therefore, I started with the long finger piece of the pinky. I'll keep all the other 2 parts of the finger the same (It means that I'll take the fingertip, fingertip connection part, and finger base, which I have designed so far), because I just have to make one part smaller so that it looks realistic and it is less work for me.

I came across a problem, because I accidentally used for the connection part of the palm a 17mm diameter (16mm is needed) at the clip mechanism connection part for the ball bearing, so I will have to do a redesign of the palm anyway.

Luckily, I recognized it today during the correction, because later on it would be much more frustrating, since I would use the same measurement next time. Maybe I will use fixed measurements to make the next redesign much easier.

So tomorrow I'll finish the pinky, maybe also a longer part for the middle finger, I don't know yet.

Most of the measurements I just copied from the old palm, so it was easier for me to make the palm.
