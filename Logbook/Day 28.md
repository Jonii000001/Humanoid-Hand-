## Pinky and middle finger designing

Today, I designed the long part for the pinky in CAD, and I also finished it for the middle finger.

I was wondering why it didn't take as long as I thought. Now I'm ready to go on, but I think I'll start programming and leave the CAD open; I'll just connect all parts and see if they fit together right.

It is a really detailed work for the housing, and I can't envision that in real life, because that is my first project and I haven't had a 3d printer so far. (I still need to design the PCB housing)

On Monday, I'll order myself the 3d printer, and hopefully it won't take long, so I can finish the CAD part and make everything work.

But my plan for now is to make a full hand assembly with the parts that I have designed so far and later to start programming, so I can treat me with a small success.

I think programming will take me much longer, because I don't want to just vibe-code stuff; instead, I'll write it from the beginning myself as a complete beginner.

But I think it's a good practice for high school and my future career as an engineering student.
