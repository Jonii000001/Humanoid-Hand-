## Creating a material list

Today I was searching for the material that I'll need for my project. I wanted to start a material list, so I can organise the hand a bit.

I also made some changes in CAD, but not much, just a few details like colliding and improving the fillets.

Today I had a lot to do in my private life, so I couldn't work for more than an hour on my project.

Finally, today I got my 3D printer. I got it faster than I thought, because I went with my dad to a provider, and we bought a used one, but in excellent condition, with only 200 hours of printing time. So, the next few days will be really exciting. First time 3D-printing.

I'm not quite sure about the materials list, whether I can buy all the things. Hopefully, I get accepted at Macondo hack club for funding (I don't have any experience with online platforms to get a price, so I don't trust it 100%), but if not, I would have to make a completely cheaper and not as professional version as this.
