## 3D Printer, socket, and cable channel mounting

Today I made great progress in the background on the project. I finally got my first 3d printer yesterday, the Bambu Lab P1S, and I had to mount it into a crate on the top of my garage, so I will have to use a ladder to get up there, but it looks really great. It is like my printing farm inside a wall.

It was a lot of work. I mounted a cable canal to the top (with rails, so you can't see the cables), and I also mounted a power socket there (my first time). It was a great experience, but it took me a very long time, because first I had to stabilise the crate, because I wanted to make sure that it wouldn't break, because of the weight.

Later, I also cleaned the 3D Printer with special sprays from Inotec and Würth (My father bought them once and had them at home). After that, I mounted a spool holder for my printer, and after like 4 hours, I had everything finished. I also know that I'll make the humanoid hand out of PLA+, because it is very strong and doesn't take so long to print. Either I take grey as a filament or black and white.
Today, I also ordered myself a 0.2mm nozzle for the printer, which will arrive on Wednesday This was, so far, the most exciting day of my life.

I couldn't take so many pictures because during work, it's difficult to film from the top, and also, I didn't want to leak anything about my home, because that wouldn't be great for my parents. So, I did it carefully.
