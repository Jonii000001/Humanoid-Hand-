Finger base shortening and PCB mounting

Today, as always, I came across a problem while I was connecting every part with the other ones (assembling the finger with the palm).

The problem was that the base finger part was too broad. So, I had to change everything, and that was a bit of work. It was in fact 1mm to broad, and this is by a humanoid hand a lot.

In addition, I added the mounting for the PCB and cut it a bit so that the PCB chip, the TLE5012B, doesn't collide with the finger part (like yesterday with the other one). The CAD program says it collides, but it can be different in real life, because it collides only 0,7mm².

The rest of this day is mostly self explained of the picture, because I can't explain every detail. It is too much. You can see from the picture that the PCBs are approx. 21mm divided from each.

I plan to print the part and then check if the part is colliding; if yes, I will change it; if not, it's great.
