Finished thumb part CAD & planning next days

Today I started with the thumb.
I designed from scratch the base part of the thumb. It took me a really long, I had no idea at the beginning how I should start, so I was looking through the whole internet to find a good mechanism for the thumb.

I had many problems in CAD and didn't know how I can design it. I wanted to use the mirror tool in Autodesk Inventor, but it glitched so much, so I had to do the design of the other side on my own for the second time, and it took me at the end 4 hours.

I was so frustrated because it robbed me of so much energy, but I'm very glad that I finished it in the end. I could put the tendon through the hole, and I also tried to see if it would work.

The next few days, I'll order my MR84ZZ ball bearing just for the prototyping, because I want to wait with the ceramic ball bearing (I will buy it for the second time, because last time I got the wrong one and also too late), because the investment is very expensive, so it has to be very well thought out before I order them.

In the next few days, I'll also go on in CAD with all finger parts and connect them. After that, I'll go on with the tendon mechanism, and at the end, I'll start the palm for the fourth time and put all tendons through a hole in the palm and connect them at the bottom.

I hope I'll get the ball bearing soon, so I can try it earlier. I also made a mistake before I printed the finger part, because I forgot the PCB mounting and the PCB ceiling.

I also didn't make the connecting part for the ball bearing shorter by 20 degrees, because 30 degrees of the clip mechanism didn't work last time, but luckily, I recognized it and changed it before I printed the part.

I forgot at the end one thing, which was that I didn't cut a bit to pass the TLE5012B through the hole on the front, like on the paper of my sketch.

My plan was for the thumb to be designed a bit curved to the right, because when I pull at the tendon, the thumb will go upwards.
