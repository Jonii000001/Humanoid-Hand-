Palm Iteration 5, printing & assembling

Today was a really productive day. I did so many things for my project.

First, I redesigned the palm for the 5th time, and this time it wasn't as bad as I thought. In fact, it was perfect for a prototype. Most of the time, I just copied the measurements from the first few tries, but I also tried some other measurements at the connection part for the ball bearing.

Then, after the long process of trying different orders of the connection parts and the different measurements. I connected all fingers (parts in CAD which I have designed so far), including pinky, ring finger, index finger, middle finger, and thumb, only with the middle part of the pinky, in technical terminology " Phalanx media," and put them on the palm.

It was really nice to see, but I ran into a problem. The base part for the pinky, pointing finger, ring finger, and middle finger was a bit too small, in fact 0,5mm so I had to stretch it 0.5mm into the width. However, I couldn't finish it today, because then I started printing the parts for the thumb.

The printing quality wasn't the best, so I had to take my technical skills and upgrade them with a cutter knife and Sandpaper and a pair of needle-nose pliers. The printing support was in every small hole, so I had to cut it out.

After that long experimenting, and correcting things (btw. I had to correct so many things, that I forgot to make the hole 0.6mm each side deeper, where the magnet fits in on the fingertip connection part).
At the end, I could show my first success. The thumb was ready and worked.

Disclaimer: My robot hand will be a bit bigger (longer) than the real one, in fact, 5 cm longer. But I can change that in the future. I only want first to get the hand working, and the tiny mechanics I can do later. Changing the part's size is much easier. than the main tendon and magnetic encoder mechanism.
