Printing fail & correcting the index finger

Today I wanted to print the palm, but I suddenly realized that I'd forgotten to add 2 holes, because I need in total 8 tendon channel holes (2 for the thumb connection part, 2 for the base, 2 for the pinky part, 2 for the fingertip connection part). I added them.

I also made some changes to the gear at the palm, and so on. Later, I wanted to print the whole palm, but it would take a bit too long (in fact, 8 hours), so I decided to print only the thumb part (a quarter of the palm) to test the whole tendon system. However, something didn't want to work.

First, I spooled 1 kg of the 5kg ecoPLA spool to a smaller spool, because otherwise it would be too heavy.
My dad showed me a way to wind up the 1kg spool from the 5kg one. We built two holders for a long bar and put the 5kg on it. On the opposite, I placed the empty 1kg spool on a drill, and then I used the drill to wind up the spool. At the end, I landed exactly at 982g filament.

I wanted to use it for test printing (in grey), but the surface was a bit strange; in fact, it was rough. Maybe that was the main problem why the printing failed. But no matter, so I stopped printing for the day. Maybe tomorrow I'll take the PLA+, which I ordered 1 month ago, and I should get the filament dryer tomorrow, and then I'll print again.

Later, I looked in my notes and came to the problem that the index finger always collided with the base (only the index finger), then I recognized that the measurements were too big. The front was 12.6mm width and it should be 11.6 mm wide. Also, the back was 1mm to big; that was the reason to design a completely new one. Otherwise, I would have to design a new base and fingertip connection part, but that would be too much work.

I was designing the new index finger part and also corrected some measurements. I also changed the size a bit, because in the original (the old one) it was 42mm long, but that was too much. In detail, it was almost as big as the middle finger part. That was the reason why I reduced the size by almost 5mm of the length, so it had a length of 38mm (body), and for reference value the pinky has 30mm (body).

In the next few days, I will go on with the fingertip and the rest of the tendon system, I'll also have the work on the PCBs (encoder and transceiver of the encoder, and maybe for the Servos). Actually, I just want to go on with the palm, but it lags so much, and my laptop can't support the performance, so I will have to move it to a later moment, when I've my new laptop and the screen protection (that will be after the vacation).

I forgot a bit about my old problem, I think, because I have two feelings that I had the problem once and didn't correct it.
