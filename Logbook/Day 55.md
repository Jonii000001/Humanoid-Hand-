Printing farm is alive again

Today, the 0.2mm nozzle arrived. I've just ordered the normal one, not the complete nozzle, because it was cheaper. At the end, I regret it a bit, because I wanted to have a spare one to change the nozzle, if one is broken or clogged with filament.

Then I completely disassembled the nozzle, which was screwed into the Bambu Lab P1S, and I saw the big filament string inside it, which was stuck in the nozzle.

So, I had no choice and disassembled the nozzle for the first time. It took me a bit because first I watched a video to make sure that I didn't break anything. I was really careful with the iron part, which I had to pull off from the nozzle to get to the heating cable, and the other one, I don't know what the other one is for, maybe I'll research now a bit. However, I used tweezers and a cotton bud; the tweezers were used to pull out the heating cable from the small hole of the stuck nozzle, and then I put the white pad away from the thermal surface.

After that, I put thermal paste on the new nozzle really carefully; I was a bit scared to break something like the old one. Furthermore, I put the heating cable into the new nozzle (the hole filled with thermal paste) and then the white pad on it. Going on, I put the iron clip's part on the nozzle again to fix the heating cable and the white pad, which lies on the thermal paste.

At the end, I put the rubber part on the front of the nozzle the rubber part.

I had only to screw the nozzle onto the printer and try to load filament. At the end, I finally fixed the printer problem, and it works, because I saw during the loading process that the filament extruded in the shape of a small string. I'm so glad!!!!!

At the end of the day, the long needle arrived, so I cleaned the nozzle for the next time, so in the future it won't happen again. Also, I have now experienced a bit of disassembling and also the problems, which the cause could be, so it'll be much easier to find the issue.

Tomorrow, the printing factory can finally go on.
