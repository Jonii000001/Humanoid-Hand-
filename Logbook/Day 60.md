## Researching transceiver

Today wasn't a special day; I just researched how I can connect all 30 magnetic encoders so that the Raspberry Pi can read the signal without having a mess of wires.

I used Gemini first for advice, but then I looked on the internet for the products. However, they were really expensive, so I asked Claude AI, and in the end I came up with the RP2400-Zero.

I also asked Claude for the connection system, and I'll have to connect the encoder SKC and Data from up to down (I have to connect all the 3 encoder from one side and all from the other side).

I also corrected some things from the product list, because yesterday I forgot to add a resistor, in fact, the 100 Ohm resistor. First, I hadn't found the 0402 100-ohm resistor from the branch Yageo, but later I found it (RS-components).

I also thought about how I should solder the resistors and capacitor onto the PCB (myself or directly to JLCMC). I'll have to look up how much I would have to pay for the soldering in the factory, but maybe much.

My soldering skills aren't the best, but I have done it approx. 3 times so far, but I think I'll nevertheless solder it myself, because it's a good practice for my next projects.
