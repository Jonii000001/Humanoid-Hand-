## Organising & CAD

Today was a logistical day.

I finished the Product bill and listed them detailed. I also looked after every part of the price and tried to find some cheaper solutions.

At the end, I changed the prices of the parts, and instead of the 12 V power cable from robotics, I'll take one from Amazon, because otherwise I would have to pay more, because of the shipping and the customs duties.

After that, I prepared everything for funding in the Macondo hack club, and after all the organising things I shipped it for funding. Hopefully it will be accepted, because it will be under review for 17 days from now.

The next few days, I'll go on in CAD when I get the right equipment (I ordered a mouse because the new laptop for school has only USB-C). Next week I won't have much time to do anything, because I will be on vacation in another country with my family and can't work so much, maybe I'll relax for one week, and when I'm at home, I'll lock in completely.

Today, at the end, I started CAD, because I will take the 4x10mm diametral magnet. I plan to close both sides of the hole and put a holding for the ball bearing there. Furthermore, in the middle of the 3d print session, I will pause the print and place the magnet inside the hole to fix it.
