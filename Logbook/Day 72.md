## Palm and thumb base refining
Today I went on with my task list from my vacation, but it didn't go as I planned because I saw a mistake. I took more TLE5012B sensors, although I didn't add the mountings for them at the bottom of the palm.

In detail, I added the mountings, and I'll have to reduce the number of TLE5012 B chips and maybe also the PCBs, so I can use the Budget for the FSR sensors.

Furthermore, I also refined the thumb base part, because there I hadn't changed the size for the diametral hole so far. This time it was a bit more difficult to change the size of the hole, because the tendon system rails were exactly in the middle of them, so I had to move them to the outside, instead of the exact middle.

At the end, I added parts to my Hand Assembly file, and I tried whether the ball bearings work, because I changed some things (I added the ball bearing holder).

In the next few days, I want to go on with my assembly instructions and the firmware of the project.
