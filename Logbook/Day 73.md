## Creating assembly instruction
Today I assembled the robotic hand in CAD; it should be my assembly instructions. I will take images and draw some things on them, show how they should be connected, and also write some details. I also found some problems and fixed them. Furthemore I made some changes to the materials, because I will reduce the price a bit (I think it doesn't have to be so uselessly expensive just for the look.

I reduced some ceramic ball bearings, so I ended up with 24 ceramic ones, and I will use 10 normal MR84ZZ, which I have ordered so far. I also reduced some TLE5012B chips and PCBs to 24 pieces. 

I wrote an item list, which I need for the assembly. 

I fixed the finger base for all 4 fingers to a smaller magnet (4x4mm), so I can use a ZZ ball bearing on the other outside, which doesn't affect the angle from the other side. 
I also didn't want to use 2 encoders on both sides, because at the front it doesn't look great and it is also bad to move, because there is always a case of the PCB at the front, and the finger couldn't move fully to the palm.
