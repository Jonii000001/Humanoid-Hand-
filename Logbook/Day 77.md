Later on, I took some time in my free time to relax, and then I came up with a smart solution.

5 PCBs on 1 finger is really hard to connect and program, so I thought of a solution. In conclusion, I will reduce the PCBs (TLE5012b chips) to only one per joint, because it‘s much easier to install and to code. It is also cheaper, because I can reduce the RP2040-Zero to 1 and the PCBs to order. I will change this in the next few days in CAD. After all, I will change the magnets to the small ones again, because I can take now at one side of the joint a MR84ZZ ball bearing I think.
