# Components

# 1) 
<img width="1498" height="769" alt="Screenshot 2026-07-30 230957" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2a912bee-1a6c-4802-a357-2da6ad36e250" />
First, you have to take the finger base part and, during the print, put the 4x4mm diametral magnet inside. 

# 2) 
<img width="711" height="609" alt="Screenshot 2026-07-30 231132" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cd0756b1-90dd-49d1-98d8-5db2439b3999" />
Second, you have to put the ball bearing, one ceramic one and one MR84ZZ (steel), on the right side of the ball bearing holders on the finger base part. The side where the holes are on the finger base part is the outside and looks at the back from the palm. The ceramic ball bearing fits on the outside where the PCB will be placed later.

# 3)
<img width="831" height="587" alt="Screenshot 2026-07-30 231559" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dc3c9db1-abd3-4193-9118-835d2d41a04a" />
Now you have to put the tendon from one side (there is a small hole to put it in) to the middle hole (there it gets led to the other side; it's just for seeing the tendon) to the other side of the base part.

# 4) 
<img width="946" height="721" alt="Screenshot 2026-07-30 231453" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7aed2699-530e-4997-b7ed-5a8b2646e894" />
Now you have to put the finger base part on the palm and be careful with the tendon (the tendon isn't visible in the picture); you have to put the tendons into the right hole through the palm to the bottom. The tendon is important for the Servomotors, because you have to know which tendon should be controlled with the Servo.

# 5)
<img width="1026" height="724" alt="Screenshot 2026-07-31 221205" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/337939f7-a609-4bad-ae73-2fc032b4415d" />
Now, you have to take the next part (pinky finger part) and put the ceramic ball bearing on both sides of it. The magnet is also already placed in the part during the print. You have to pause the print when the hole for the magnet is 3/4 closed, then place the magnet (this time the 4x10mm) inside the part and resume the print.
The green and the red line should visualize the tendons. The red one is the tendon, which goes in a circle in the part back to the Servo, so you can exactly control the part on its own. The green one is from the part above.

# 6) 
<img width="732" height="435" alt="Screenshot 2026-07-31 221636" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/52cdffb6-bdad-4741-b3a8-bb8bd2ef6ec2" />
Now you have to take the fingertip connection part, put it on both sides of the ceramic ball bearings, and then connect it on the pinky part to the rest of the finger. The green tendon now visualizes the controlling tendon for the part, as I said before, "circle system".
(The magnet is already placed inside the part)

# 7) 
<img width="1348" height="755" alt="Screenshot 2026-07-31 222327" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f3bc1464-0fdd-4d8b-8c2c-08454cf92e3f" />
Now, you have to use an M2 Screw to screw the fingertip to the fingertip connection part.

# 8)
<img width="925" height="390" alt="Screenshot 2026-07-31 222929" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/293fe014-b724-4d59-b4d2-c1383fa88e58" />
This hole on every part is to fix the tendon so that it can't slip through and so that the Servomotor can pull the part. You can fix the tendon with a threaded rod or just a stick (the hole has a diameter of 2mm). The green lines visualize the tendons inside the part.

# 9)
<img width="1118" height="682" alt="Screenshot 2026-07-31 223548" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/001ea2be-6bae-45be-9b36-3a9ba5b868a0" />
Lastly, you have to put the PCB on the PCB holders on each side and above each PCB, the cover (you just have to pull it up there, without a screw or anything), like in the image you can see the green ones are for the side. Two components for every side (PCB + PCB cover), and the red one is for the backside of the palm (where the ceramic ball bearing is placed)

# 1)

# 2)
<img width="1005" height="619" alt="Screenshot 2026-08-01 152851" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/64a2f82f-9fef-4d4e-8c9b-5eeaaef8a825" />

# 3)
<img width="542" height="433" alt="Screenshot 2026-08-01 153046" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fc65d2e9-e272-4b88-bc07-f931c2670e39" />

# 4)
<img width="421" height="286" alt="Screenshot 2026-08-01 153219" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1a9c3d6d-3daa-408c-bb28-c0354d365233" />
