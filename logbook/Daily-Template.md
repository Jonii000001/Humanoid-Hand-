# Daily Entry Template

Use this template for every new daily log. Copy it to logbook/Day-XXX-YYYY-MM-DD.md and fill the sections.

---
Day: Day XX — YYYY-MM-DD
Time spent: H:MM

TL;DR (1 line)
- Short summary & result.

Objective
- What I planned to do today.

What I did
- Bullet list of actions (CAD, prints, purchases, firmware, tests).

Outcome / Results
- What worked, what didn't, measurements, files produced.

Technical notes
- Exact numbers: tolerances, part numbers, magnet sizes, temperatures, slicer settings.

Decisions & reasoning
- Why you changed something or kept it.

Feelings
- One sentence about how you felt (proud, tired, frustrated, excited).

Next steps (3 items)
- Prioritized next actions.

Images / attachments
- Embed images with the exact links (don't modify them). Example:
  <img width="1200" alt="Screenshot 2026-05-21 191616" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d0148b4d-d4ed-425a-b457-86452a322930" />
